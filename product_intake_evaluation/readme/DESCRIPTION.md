Products can be registered in Odoo by portal users and then evaluated and managed.

This is intended for operations such as second-hand businesses, where products are registered in advance by portal users, and only accepted products are brought into the second-hand warehouse.

Internal users can review the product information registered by portal users (images, condition, comments, etc.) 
and update the valuation status.
Portal users can check that status and confirm whether their product has been marked as acceptable. 
Only acceptable products are actually delivered.

The user access rights for the Intake Evaluation feature are as follows:

1. Intake Evaluation Admin: Assigned to internal users who manage the Intake Evaluation functionality.
2. Intake Evaluation Manager: Portal user role that allows viewing of all products registered by all portal users.
3. Intake Evaluation User: Portal user role that allows viewing only of products registered by themselves.

This module depends on the OCA base_group_backend module.
