To show “Add Custom Filter and "Add Custom Group" for specific users:

- Go to Settings → Users & Companies → Groups.
- Search for "Custom Filter and Group By Access".
- Open the group and add the desired users.

To show “Add Custom Filter and "Add Custom Group" for all users in a specific group:

- Go to Settings → Users & Companies → Groups.
- Search and open the group you want to grant access to.
- Under the Inherited tab, add "Custom Filter and Group By Access" group.
